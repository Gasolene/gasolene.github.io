FlipStudy Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This notice describes FlipStudy Index Cards, operated by A2DWebs, including version 4.4.8.

Your cards and study settings

Your card text, drawings, selected photos, topics and study settings are stored on your device. If you enable the app’s optional iCloud synchronization, Apple processes that content through your iCloud account. New topic sharing uses a portable .flipstudy file through the system share sheet: you choose the recipient or service. The file can contain the topic’s card text, images and formatting, so share it only with recipients you intend to receive that content. Recipients and services may retain copies outside your control.

Google advertising and diagnostics

The ad-supported app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Google may receive device or app-scoped identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, advertising and interaction information, crash information, performance measurements and other technical diagnostics. These support advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention and service operation. Information associated with an identifier may be linked to your device even when we do not know your name.

Firebase Crashlytics also processes crash reports and installation identifiers to help diagnose failures. General Firebase Analytics event collection is disabled in version 4.4.8. The app does not currently request access to Apple’s advertising identifier through an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Advertising identifiers remain subject to iOS permission restrictions; an ad-free purchase removes the app’s advertising.

Firebase and older sharing features

The app retains Firebase services for compatibility with older shared-topic links and app configuration. Those services can process network and technical information and anonymous account identifiers associated with legacy sharing. Earlier versions could upload shared topics and images to Firebase; previously shared copies may remain there. Version 4.4.8 uses local topic files for new sharing rather than uploading new shared topics through the retired Dynamic Links feature. No new email-and-password account is required to use the app.

Purchases, permissions and support

Apple handles in-app payment and purchase restoration. We do not receive your payment-card or bank details. The app stores purchase entitlements locally to enable purchased features. Camera and photo-library access are used when you choose to add or save images. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond.

Your choices and data requests

Where required and available, the app presents Google consent messages and a Privacy options control. You can manage device permissions and iCloud settings through Apple’s settings. You can delete local topics and cards in the app; copies you shared, iCloud copies and provider-held diagnostics may have separate retention and deletion processes. Contact us about legacy shared content or other privacy requests. Google and Apple administer data held by their services under their own retention policies.

Third-party services and external links

Google and Apple process service data under their respective privacy notices. If you open an advertisement or external website, that destination has its own privacy practices.

Contact and changes

For questions or privacy requests, contact support@a2dwebs.com. We will update this notice when relevant app practices change.
