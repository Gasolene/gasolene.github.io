A2DWebs drawing apps — Privacy notice

Last updated November 16, 2026.

This notice covers How To Draw Eyes with Steps, How To Draw Cats & Kittens, How To Draw Dogs and Puppies, How To Draw Emojis & Emoticons, How to Draw Farm Animals Fun, How to Draw Horses with Steps, How To Draw Lips with Steps, and How To Draw with Steps (Universal), operated by A2DWebs.

Drawing and device settings

The apps let you follow drawing lessons and save your drawings to your device. The drawing apps do not upload your saved drawings or photo library to A2DWebs. The apps store settings on your device, such as purchased drawing packs, ad-free status and whether a rating prompt has appeared. Apple processes App Store payments; the apps do not receive your payment-card information.

Advertising and consent services

The apps include Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process device identifiers, approximate location (including location inferred from an IP address), advertising and app interactions, crash information, performance information and other diagnostic information. These services use data for advertising, analytics and operating their services. Some data can be associated with a device, and advertising identifiers may be used for tracking where permitted by device settings, permissions and applicable consent requirements.

Where required and available, the app presents Google's consent form and offers a Privacy choices control. You can also manage advertising and tracking permissions through your device settings. The availability of advertising identifiers depends on your operating system and permissions.

For Google's practices, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's explanation of information collected by its iOS advertising SDK.

Additional services in Universal

How To Draw with Steps (Universal) also uses Google Firebase for its drawing catalogue, optional email/password accounts, drawing credits, analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring. When you use an account, Firebase processes your email address and account credentials to authenticate you. Account or installation identifiers and credit, purchase and unlock records are used to operate the service and associate your available drawing credits with your account or device. Firebase also processes usage, performance and diagnostic information. Other drawing apps covered by this notice do not offer this Firebase-backed account and credit system.

For Firebase's processing and retention practices, see Privacy and Security in Firebase. A2DWebs does not specify a fixed automatic deletion period for Universal account and credit records in this notice. Contact us through the support link on the app's App Store page for questions about your account or to request deletion of information held by A2DWebs.

Your choices and requests

You can manage device permissions in Settings and use any privacy controls presented in the app. Removing an app removes its ordinary local app data, subject to Apple's device-backup behavior; it does not itself delete Firebase account or credit records. Contact A2DWebs through the app's App Store support link for privacy questions, account-data requests, or questions concerning a child's use of the apps.

The current public support page is A2DWebs on Facebook.

Updates

A2DWebs may update this notice to reflect changes to the apps and their services. The published notice will display its latest update date.
