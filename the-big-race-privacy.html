The Big Race — Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This notice covers A2DWebs’ English-language iPad app, The Big Race (App Store ID 1025371209), including its restored version 1.3.

Data collection

The Big Race does not collect or transmit personal data to A2DWebs or to third-party advertising or analytics providers. It has no accounts, advertisements, in-app purchases, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Its story, illustrations, animations and music are bundled in the app and can be used offline.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your selected music and volume settings locally on your device. These preferences are used to remember your settings and are not transmitted to A2DWebs. Deleting the app removes its ordinary local data, subject to Apple’s device backup and restoration features.

Children and external links

The storybook is designed for children and does not ask them to provide personal information. A parent gate appears before opening external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information when an adult chooses to visit them. The app does not include an embedded web browser or send app-usage records to these websites.

Apple services

Apple operates the App Store, TestFlight, device backups and optional platform diagnostics under Apple’s own privacy policies and your device settings. This notice describes the app’s own operation.

Contact and updates

For support or privacy questions, contact A2DWebs through its existing support page: A2DWebs support . Please do not post a child’s personal information publicly.

We will update this notice if the app’s practices change.
